\subsection{Motivation}
\label{sec:motivation}
\begin{itemize}
	\item Cost-optimal \acp{ESM} return a single least-cost solution.
	\item Decision makers care about more than the optimum: robustness, which
	technologies are ``almost'' competitive, and what a constraint costs at the
	margin.
	\item LP duality already encodes this in \aclp{RC} and \aclp{SP}, but
	\acp{ESM} typically discard it.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Contributions}
\label{sec:contributions}
\begin{itemize}
	\item Review of how boundary information is handled in established \acp{ESM}.
	\item A post-processing pipeline that extracts \aclp{SP} and \aclp{RC} from
	ZEN-garden.
	\item A capex-equivalent reduced-cost metric for investment variables.
	\item A characterisation of the numerical pitfalls (degeneracy, presolve,
	scaling) that make naive dual readings unreliable, with mitigations.
\end{itemize}
