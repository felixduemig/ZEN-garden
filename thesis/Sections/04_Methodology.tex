\subsection{Capex-Equivalent Reduced Cost from the Lifetime Dual}
\label{sec:capex-equivalent}
\begin{itemize}
	\item The native solver \ac{RC} of \texttt{capacity\_addition} is in
	annualised objective units and is often zero for degenerate-basic variables.
	\item Instead, reconstruct an investment-level signal from the dual of the
	lifetime coupling constraint.
\end{itemize}

The capex-equivalent reduced cost is
\begin{equation}
	\label{eq:rc-capex}
	\text{rc\_capex\_equivalent}
	= \text{capex\_specific}
	- \frac{\sum_{} \left(-\,\text{dual}_\text{lifetime}\right)}
	       {\text{annuity}\,\cdot\,\sum_{} \text{discount\_factor}} ,
\end{equation}
reported in input units ($\si{\text{\euro}\per\kilo\watt}$).
\begin{itemize}
	\item Derive the annuity / \ac{CRF} factor.
	\item Show the bit-exact identity for a marginally-built technology
	(lifetime dual $=-(\text{capex}\cdot\text{CRF})$); full derivation in
	Appendix~\ref{app:rc-derivation}.
\end{itemize}

%---------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Shadow-Price Extraction and the Generic-Technology Trick}
\label{sec:shadow-price}
\begin{itemize}
	\item Shadow prices are read directly from constraint duals.
	\item To isolate the marginal value of a specific boundary, insert a
	\emph{generic technology} whose single defining constraint exposes the
	desired \ac{SP} in isolation.
\end{itemize}
