\subsection{Test Models}
\label{sec:test-models}
We evaluate the implementation on two models.
\begin{itemize}
	\item \textbf{Toy LP.} A small, hand-checkable model where \ac{RC}/\ac{SP}
	are known in closed form. Used to verify correctness, including the
	perturbation check (reducing a technology's \ac{CAPEX} by exactly its
	capex-equivalent \ac{RC} brings it to the build margin).
	\item \textbf{Crystal Ball.} The 28-node European instance
	(Section~\ref{sec:crystal-ball}). Used to apply the method at scale, with
	cloned/perturbed technologies to test the ``\ac{CAPEX} $-$ \ac{RC} $\to$ just
	gets built'' behaviour.
\end{itemize}
